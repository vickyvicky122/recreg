How we measured it

No model to train. We ran the same matching code that ships in the extension.

Step 1

Write 200 titles

Realistic video titles for each preset, plus everyday titles and tricky ones that should stay visible.

Step 2

Label each one

Our team marked every title "should hide" or "should stay visible". Disagreements are noted.

Step 3

Tune on half

We adjusted the preset words using 100 of the titles.

Step 4

Report, with a caveat

The numbers on this page come from those same 100 titles. A fair test needs titles the presets have never seen.

What this does not show

  • These numbers come from the titles we tuned the presets on, so they are optimistic. They are not an independent test.
  • We only check titles. Thumbnails, descriptions and captions are not read.
  • All titles are in English and written by us. Real feeds are messier.
  • Word matching misses changed spellings and titles with no keyword.
  • Labels are human judgement, and people can disagree.
