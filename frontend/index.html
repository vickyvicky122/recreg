Your feed. Your rules.

recreg is a browser extension that gives you control over recommended content on YouTube, Reddit and Pinterest. You define what you want to avoid, and recreg hides it before you see it, no matter what the algorithm thinks you watched.

Interacting with content is not consent to future recommendations.

How it works

Three steps, and you stay in charge of every one of them.

Step 1

Choose your boundaries

Pick topic presets or add your own words and phrases: spoilers, gambling, a phobia, a news event you need a break from. You never have to explain why.

Step 2

Pick your sites

Enable recreg per site. It only gets access to the sites you choose, and asks your browser for permission one site at a time.

Step 3

Browse normally

As recommendations load, recreg cross-checks each card against your rules on your device and covers matches before they render. Reveal any item with one click. Your own searches stay untouched.

Supported sites today: YouTube Reddit Pinterest More coming.

Get recreg

recreg is heading to the Chrome Web Store. Until it is listed, install it in about a minute.

Download extension
  1. Download the extension and unzip it.
  2. Open chrome://extensions and turn on Developer mode.
  3. Click Load unpacked and select the unzipped folder.
  4. Open recreg's settings, enable your sites, and set your filters.

Works on Chrome, Edge, Brave and Arc. Supported sites today: YouTube, Reddit and Pinterest, with more coming.

Private by architecture, not by promise

What you filter is your business.

No servers

Your filter list stays on your device in browser storage. There is no account, no sync, no backend to breach.

No network access

The extension requests zero network capability. It cannot send your data anywhere, and you can verify that by reading its manifest.

No tracking

No analytics, no telemetry, no crash reporting. recreg does not know what you filter, and neither does anyone else.

Least privilege

Site access is granted per site, by you, only for the sites you enable. The code is open source and auditable.
